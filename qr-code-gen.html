ABOUT THIS TOOL

Free Online QR Code Generator

A QR code is a convenient way to share information using a smartphone camera. With the TAHSION QR Code Generator, you can quickly create a QR code for a website, text, phone number, email address or Wi-Fi network.

The tool works directly in your browser, making it fast and convenient for everyday use. You don't need to create an account or provide personal information to generate a QR code.

What can you create?

  • Website and URL QR codes
  • Plain text QR codes
  • Phone number QR codes
  • Email QR codes
  • Wi-Fi connection QR codes
  • Custom colored QR codes
