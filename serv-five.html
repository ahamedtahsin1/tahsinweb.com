Launching your website is not the finish line. Software changes, security threats evolve, dependencies break, servers need attention and even a small technical issue can turn into lost leads, failed transactions or downtime.

Our Technical Maintenance & Support service gives your business an experienced engineering team behind the platform after launch. We continuously maintain, monitor and improve the systems that keep your website running.

Stay operational. Stay ahead.

Instead of waiting for something to break, we take a proactive approach to website maintenance. Security updates, backups, performance checks, technical fixes and infrastructure issues are handled before they become bigger business problems.

Whether we built your website or inherited an existing platform, our engineers can work across the technical stack to keep your digital presence stable, secure and ready to grow.

  • Website health monitoring
  • Security monitoring and hardening
  • Software, CMS and dependency updates
  • Automated backups and recovery support
  • Bug fixing and technical troubleshooting
  • Server and hosting support
  • Performance optimization
  • Database maintenance and optimization
  • SSL, DNS and domain configuration support
  • Third-party API and integration fixes
  • Post-launch feature development
  • Ongoing technical consulting

Technical problems shouldn't become business problems.

Your team should not have to search for a developer every time something goes wrong. With an ongoing technical partner, you have a team that already understands your platform and can diagnose problems quickly, make informed changes and keep the system moving forward.

step 01

audit & understand

We first understand your existing website, hosting environment, CMS, integrations, dependencies and current technical risks. This gives us a clear baseline before making changes.

step 02

maintain & protect

We keep the platform updated, backed up and monitored while addressing security risks, technical issues and performance bottlenecks before they impact your users.

step 03

improve & evolve

As your business changes, your website should change with it. We handle ongoing improvements, new features, integrations and technical upgrades without losing sight of stability.
