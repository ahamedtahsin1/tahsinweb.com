What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The percentage symbol (%) means "out of 100." Percentages are commonly used for discounts, price changes, test scores, business growth, statistics, taxes, and everyday calculations.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, divide the percentage by 100 and multiply it by the number.

Percentage × Number ÷ 100

For example, to calculate 20% of 500:

20 × 500 ÷ 100 = 100

How to Find What Percentage One Number Is of Another

Divide the first number by the second number and multiply the result by 100.

(First Number ÷ Second Number) × 100

For example:

(100 ÷ 500) × 100 = 20%

How to Calculate Percentage Increase or Decrease

Percentage change compares an original value with a new value. Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.

((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

A positive result means the value increased. A negative result means the value decreased.
