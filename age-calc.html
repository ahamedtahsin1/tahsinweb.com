HOW IT WORKS

Calculate your exact age

Our age calculator calculates the difference between your date of birth and the selected calculation date. The result is shown in years, months and days.

You can also see your approximate total number of days lived and how many days remain until your next birthday.

FAQ

Age Calculator Questions

Can I calculate my age for a past date?

Yes. Change the "Calculate age as of" date to any date after your birth date.

Does the calculator require an account?

No. TAHSION Free Tools are designed to work without signup or an account.

What does the calculator show?

It shows your age in years, months and days, total days lived, and the countdown to your next birthday.
